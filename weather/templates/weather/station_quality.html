Temperature calibration

Correct a temperature sensor that reads wrong in a consistent way, such as a poorly shielded sensor that runs warm in sunshine and cold on clear nights. The correction uses the time of day and the solar radiation, month by month. Original readings are kept; removing a calibration restores them.
