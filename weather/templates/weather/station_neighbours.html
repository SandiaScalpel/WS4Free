Compare your temperature and humidity with nearby stations on Weather Underground. Their current conditions are fetched every {{ poll_minutes }} minutes and matched to your readings for the same five minutes. The comparison averages the neighbours after leaving out the highest and the lowest reading, so one badly placed sensor doesn't skew it. Only those who manage this station (or are advanced viewers of it) can see this page.

For calibration

Work out a temperature correction from how your sensor differs from the neighbours over a period you choose. Pick days when your sensor was where it is now; a mix of sunny days and clear nights shows its errors best. The fit uses your sensor's own readings, before any calibration, and only the neighbours that are counted.

Neighbour readings are available from {{ fc.first|date:"M j, Y" }}. Removing a neighbour removes its readings too.
