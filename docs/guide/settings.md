# Settings and sharing

## Station settings

Owners reach these from **Manage** on any station page.

- **Visibility**: **Public** lets anyone view the station without signing in;
  **Private** shows it only to you. Settings, the upload address and the upload
  log are never public.
- **Name** and **place** ("Boulder, Colorado"), shown at the top of every page.
- **Time zone**: defines the station's local day, so daily highs and lows,
  rain totals and the midnight rain reset follow your clock. Changing it
  recalculates the daily summaries in the background.
- **Latitude, longitude and elevation**: used for evapotranspiration and to
  work out when the sun is up. Elevation is entered in feet or metres to match
  your units.
- **Wind sensor height**: used to adjust wind to the standard 2 m height for
  evapotranspiration.
- **Data sources**: whether to fill gaps from ambientweather.net (Ambient
  stations), and which **rain sensor** to record when the console has two.
- **Extra sensors**: names and public switches; see
  [Extra sensors](extra-sensors.md).

The **Console & uploads** tab has the upload address and log (see
[Connecting a station](connecting.md)); **Data quality** has exclusions and
calibration (see [Data quality and calibration](data-quality.md)).

## Site settings

Administrators can set the site-wide options under **Site settings** in the menu
under their name:

- **Site title**: shown in the header and browser tab;
- **Tagline**: a short line beside the title in the header (on wide screens);
- **About**: a few sentences for visitors;
- **Default station**: the one the home page shows when there are several.

## Your account

**Settings & security**, in the menu under your name:

- **Appearance**: light, dark, or automatic;
- **Units**: per quantity (see [Getting started](getting-started.md#units));
- **Security**: change your password, set up an authenticator app, and register
  passkeys.
