# Reports

The **Reports** tab summarises any period as a table you can read on screen or
download.

## Choosing what to show

- **Period**: this month, last month, this year, last year, everything, or
  **Custom** dates.
- **Rows**: one per day, month or year; *Automatic* picks one that fits the
  period.
- **Columns**: choose from temperature (high, low, mean, average high and low),
  heating, cooling and growing degree days, hot and freeze days, humidity, dew
  point, rain, rain days, peak rain rate, ET₀, wind, gust, pressure, peak solar,
  UV, and days with data.

A **whole period** row at the bottom totals or averages each column.
**Download CSV** saves the same table.

## Conventions

Reports follow the US National Weather Service's monthly climate summaries:

| | Definition |
|---|---|
| Degree days | from (high + low) ÷ 2, base 65 °F (18 °C) |
| Hot day | high of 90 °F (32 °C) or more |
| Freeze day | low of 32 °F (0 °C) or less |
| Rain day | 0.01 in (0.25 mm) or more |

Means, degree days and day counts only use days with at least 90% of their
readings, so an outage doesn't make a month look mild. Highs, lows and rain
use every reading there is. **Days with data** shows how complete each row is,
and rows with missing days are marked
**partial**.
