# Almanac

The **Almanac** tab collects the station's history into the things people ask
about the weather.

## On this day

Pick a date (today by default) to see it in every year the station has
recorded: the high, low, rain and peak gust each year (with a link to that
day's chart), the station's own **normal** high
and low for the date (averaged over the three days either side, every year),
and the record high and low for that date.

## Records

All-time records, or one year's, from the menu:

- highest and lowest temperature, with the time they happened;
- lowest wind chill and highest heat index, with the time;
- warmest night (highest daily low) and coldest day (lowest daily high);
- wettest day and wettest month, heaviest rain rate;
- strongest gust;
- highest and lowest pressure;
- highest UV;
- longest dry spell.

Measured extremes count even on a day with gaps. Whole-day records (warmest
night, coldest day) need at least 90% of the day recorded, so a day the sensor
was offline can't set a false record. A dry spell ends at any day without data.

If a record looks wrong, the owner sees **Exclude these readings…** beside it,
which opens [Data quality](data-quality.md) with that day filled in.

## Frost dates

The last frost of spring and the first of fall each year, the frost-free
season between them, and the averages. **Frost** means a low of 32 °F (0 °C) or
colder; **Hard freeze** means 28 °F (−2.2 °C).

A date marked **uncertain** means the station missed part of a night when a
frost could have changed the answer. Uncertain years aren't used in the
averages.
