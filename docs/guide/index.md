# WS4Free user guide

WS4Free keeps your personal weather station's readings on your own server and
turns them into a live dashboard, charts, records, reports and growing
statistics, without a cloud subscription.

This guide is for the people who use a WS4Free site: station owners, and
visitors to a public station. Installing and running the server is covered in
the [README](../../README.md).

## Where to start

- **New here?** [Getting started](getting-started.md) explains signing in, the
  units and theme switches, and what visitors can see.
- **Setting up a station?** [Connecting a station](connecting.md) covers Ambient
  Weather and Ecowitt consoles, WeeWX, and importing your history.
- **Something looks wrong?** [Data quality and calibration](data-quality.md)
  shows how to set aside a failing sensor's readings, or correct a sensor that
  reads too warm or too cold.

## The pages

| Page | What it's for |
|---|---|
| [Dashboard](dashboard.md) | Current conditions, today's highs and lows, rain totals and 24-hour trends |
| [Charts](charts.md) | Any date range, wind rose, calendar heatmap, year-over-year comparisons |
| [Almanac](almanac.md) | This day in past years, records, first and last frost |
| [Growing](growing.md) | Growing degree days, winter chill, evapotranspiration |
| [Reports](reports.md) | Daily, monthly and yearly summaries with CSV export |
| [Extra sensors](extra-sensors.md) | Extra temperature channels, soil, leaf wetness, air quality, lightning, leaks |
| [Settings and sharing](settings.md) | Station settings, public or private, site settings, your account |
| [How the numbers work](how-it-works.md) | Rain counting, missing data, time zones and summaries |

Each station page has a **Help** link at the right of its tabs that opens the
matching section of this guide.
