# Getting started

## Visitors, owners and the people they share with

Anyone can view a **public** station without an account: its dashboard,
charts, almanac, growing and reports pages. A **private** station is visible
only to its owner, the people they share it with, and the site's administrators.

Owners sign in to manage their stations. Some things are never shown to
visitors, even on a public station:

- indoor temperature and humidity;
- extra sensors the owner hasn't made public (see [Extra sensors](extra-sensors.md));
- low-battery warnings;
- the station's settings, upload address and upload log.

An owner can share a station with other users as a **viewer**, an **advanced
viewer** or a **manager**; see [Users and access](users.md). Once you're signed
in, the home page shows your own station if you have one.

## Signing in

Use **Sign in** at the top right. WS4Free requires two-step sign-in: after
your password, confirm with an authenticator app (a six-digit code) or a
**passkey** (your phone, a security key, or a password manager). If you haven't
set one up yet, you're reminded after signing in. Add or remove them under
**Settings & security** in the menu under your name.

For security, you're signed out after two hours without activity. A dashboard
left open keeps updating, but that doesn't count as activity.

## Units

The **°F / °C** switch in the header changes everything between US and metric
units. Signed-in users can mix units, for example °F with hPa and millimetres,
under **Settings & security → Units**:

| Quantity | Choices |
|---|---|
| Temperature | °F, °C |
| Wind | mph, km/h, m/s, knots |
| Pressure | inHg, hPa, mmHg |
| Rain | inches, millimetres |

Readings are always stored in metric units, so switching never changes your
data, only how it's shown. CSV downloads use the units you're viewing.

## Light and dark

The moon/sun button in the header switches between light and dark. Signed-in
users can also choose **Automatic** (follow the device) under **Settings &
security → Appearance**; the choice follows you to any device you sign in on.

## Finding your way around

- The site's name in the header goes to the home page: the site's main
  station, or a list of stations when the site has several and no main one is
  set.
- **Stations** in the header, shown when there's more than one station you can
  see, drops down a list of them to choose from; **All stations** at the bottom
  opens the full list. On a station page, **Switch station** beside the station's
  name opens another station on the same tab.
- Site administrators also find **Manage stations** in their user menu, beside
  **Site settings**: the list of every station, with **Add a station**, even
  when the site has only one. **Users** adds and manages accounts (see
  [Users and access](users.md)).
- **Help**, at the top right, opens this guide.
- Each station has tabs: **Overview**, **Charts**, **Almanac**, **Growing** and
  **Reports**. Owners, managers and advanced viewers (read-only) also see
  **Manage**, which leads to **Settings**, **Console & uploads**, **Data
  quality** and more; owners also get **People**.
- Most pages keep their choices in the address (a date range, a report period),
  so you can bookmark or share exactly what you see.
